Moon pancakes🌙

The easiest breakfast in the galasy!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 1 spoon of sugar

How to make them

  1. Mix all of the ingredients in the bowl
  2. Pour a little mix in a warm pan
  3. Cook both sides until golden
  4. Add your favorite topping

Chef's tip : add a banana for extra happiness.🍌

Enjoy your pancakes!
